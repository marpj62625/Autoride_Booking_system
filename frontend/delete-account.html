Request Account Deletion

Manage your Autoride account and data preferences

If you no longer wish to use Autoride, you can request to delete your account and all associated personal data. Below, you will find instructions on how to submit a request and what happens to your data.

How to Delete Your Account

You can choose either of these two methods to submit a deletion request:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Instant Request)

  1. Open the Autoride mobile application.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Edit Profile.
  4. Click the Delete Account button.
  5. Confirm your password and confirm the deletion.

Method 2: Web / Email Request

If you have already uninstalled the app and want to delete your account without reinstalling, please email us directly:

Send an email to: support@autoride.com
Subject: "Autoride Account Deletion Request"

Please send the email using the address associated with your Autoride account. We will process your deletion request within 48-72 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

Upon processing your account deletion request, the following information will be permanently deleted from our databases:

  • Your account profile (Full name, Email Address, and Phone Number).
  • Your stored password credentials.
  • All uploaded identity verification photos, including your Driver's License image.

What Data is Kept, and for How Long?

To comply with business accounting, tax, and legal liability requirements, the following data is retained temporarily:

  • Booking and Financial Logs: Transaction and ride records are stored securely for up to 1 year to settle audits, address dispute resolutions, or comply with financial reporting laws.
  • After this period, financial transactions are fully anonymized or permanently erased.
