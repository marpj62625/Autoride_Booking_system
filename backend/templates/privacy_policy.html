Privacy Policy for Autoride

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Autoride. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our car rental mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture when you register
  • Authentication Data: Google Sign-In credentials for secure authentication
  • Booking Information: Rental dates, vehicle selection, pickup/return locations
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates for vehicle tracking and service delivery (with your permission)
  • Payment Information: Transaction records (payment processing is handled by secure third-party providers)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app usage data
  • Communication Data: Messages and notifications related to your bookings

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and manage your car rental bookings
  • Authenticate and secure your account
  • Provide customer support and respond to your inquiries
  • Send booking confirmations, reminders, and important updates
  • Track vehicle location for operational purposes
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services for payment processing, SMS notifications, and push notifications
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Partners: With your consent for specific services

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Secure HTTPS connections
  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure authentication using Google Sign-In
  • Regular security updates and monitoring

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking
  • Opt-out of marketing communications

7. Location Services

Our app uses location services to:

  • Track rented vehicles in real-time
  • Provide accurate pickup and return location information
  • Enhance your booking experience

You can disable location services in your device settings, but this may limit app functionality.

8. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with third-party services including:

  • Google Sign-In for authentication
  • Firebase for push notifications
  • SMS providers for text notifications
  • Payment gateways for secure transactions

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Booking history is retained for record-keeping purposes.
